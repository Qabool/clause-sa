Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information CLAUSE Statistical Assessment (“CLAUSE”, “the app”), developed by Qabool Ahmed (“the developer”, “we”), handles when you use it.

In short: your account and your reports stay on your own device. We do not collect, receive or sell your data, we do not run advertising or analytics in this version, and we do not use cookies for tracking.

1. Information the app stores on your device

When you use CLAUSE it stores the following in the app’s private storage on your device (the browser database used by the installed app):

  • Account details: your User ID and full name. Your password and the answers to your security questions are stored only as salted, one-way cryptographic hashes; the app cannot show them to you or to us.
  • Your work: variables and measurement-unit settings, logged data you enter or import, computed results, and the draft and completed reports you create, including the recycle bin (deleted reports are kept for 30 days).
  • Plan and usage: your current plan (Free or Pro), when it was last verified, and weekly usage counters used to apply the Free plan limits.
  • Convenience data: your last-used User ID (to pre-fill the sign-in box) and, while the window is open, a session marker. If you tick “Keep me logged in”, the app remembers that you are signed in on this device.

This information is not sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or usage statistics.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks or third-party tracking cookies in this version of the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not receive any.

3. Network connections the app makes

  • Loading and updating the app. The app is delivered as a web app. Its files are loaded from the website that hosts them, which, like any web server, may record technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. The app can then work offline on the Pro plan; the Free plan requires an internet connection.
  • Microsoft Store. When installed from the Microsoft Store, the app asks the Store which subscriptions you own and, if you choose to upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by Microsoft. We never see your payment details. Microsoft’s privacy statement applies to that processing.

4. Backups and exports

You can export a backup file and export reports (PDF, Excel). These files are created on your device and are yours to store or share. A backup file contains your account record, including the salted hashes described above, so keep it somewhere secure.

5. Security

Data is kept in your device’s app storage and is protected by your device’s own security (for example your Windows sign-in and disk encryption). CLAUSE does not encrypt your reports with your password, and anyone who can use your unlocked device may be able to access the app’s data. Please lock your device and keep backups.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual reports (they move to the recycle bin for 30 days), empty the recycle bin, or use Profile → Delete account and all data to erase your account, reports and settings from the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data for you.

7. Children

CLAUSE is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and will use them only to reply to you. Contact: connect.proffsen@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app later adds features that change how information is handled (for example cloud sync, advertising or analytics), we will update this policy and the date above before those features are released.
